## FAQ

**How do I create a new alarm group?**  
Tap the "+" button in the top right app and enter a title for your schedule. Add alarms to the schedule and set their titles, times or relative positions.

**How do I enable and disable schedules?**  
Swipe left on a schedule from the main list and hit 'Disable' or 'Enable'. You can also delete from there. 

**How do I adjust multiple alarms at once?**  
Change the time of the linked alarm to affect both alarms. For example, if 'Time to Leave' is set 10 minutes before 'Start Work', you only need to change the time of 'Start Work'. 'Time to Leave' will adjust automatically.
